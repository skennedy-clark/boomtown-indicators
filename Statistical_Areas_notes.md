# A Note on Statistical Areas

These areas are based on the **Australian Statistical Geography Standard
(ASGS) Edition 3**.

## Statistical Area Level 1 (SA1)

Statistical Areas Level 1 (SA1s) are geographic areas built from whole
Mesh Blocks. Whole SA1s aggregate to form Statistical Areas Level 2
(SA2s). SA1s are designed to maximise the geographic detail available
for Census of Population and Housing data. Limited Census data may also
be available at the Mesh Block level.

-   **Total regions:** 61,845\
-   Includes **34 non-spatial special purpose codes** (e.g. *Outside
    Australia*, *Migratory*, *No Usual Address*)

### Purpose

SA1s: - Cover all of Australia without gaps or overlaps\
- Act as building blocks for: - Indigenous Structure - Significant Urban
Areas - Urban Centres and Localities - Section of State Structure -
Remoteness Structure

### Design Criteria

#### Population

-   Typically **200--800 people**
-   Average: **\~400**
-   Smaller in remote/regional areas

#### Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Population

-   Designed to accurately represent communities, especially in remote
    areas

#### Urban vs Rural

**Urban SA1s may include:** - Dense residential (\>200 people/km²) -
Infrastructure (ports, airports, industry, retail, etc.) - Parks, sports
facilities, schools, religious sites - Utilities and transport hubs

**Rural SA1s may include:** - Low-density residential (\<200
people/km²) - Agriculture, mines, defence land - National parks and
undeveloped land

#### Transport

-   Generally internally connected by roads\
-   Exceptions: islands

#### Suburbs and Localities

-   Prefer alignment with gazetted suburbs/localities\
-   Small localities may be grouped

#### Growth

-   Designed to accommodate expected development

#### Special Cases

-   **Prisons / detention centres (\>200 people):** separate SA1\
-   **Defence bases (\>200 people):** separate SA1

#### Zero SA1s (nil population)

Used for large unpopulated areas such as: - Airports, ports, industrial
zones - National parks, deserts - Large infrastructure or restricted
land - Groups of unpopulated islands

## Statistical Area Level 2 (SA2)

SA2s are medium-sized areas built from SA1s and represent communities
that interact socially and economically.

-   **Total regions:** 2,473\
-   Includes **19 non-spatial codes**

### Purpose

-   Smallest geography for most ABS non-Census statistics\
-   Aggregates to SA3

### Design Criteria

#### Population

-   Typically **3,000--25,000**
-   Average: **\~10,000**

#### Functional Areas

-   Based on service access regions (towns, cities, hubs)

#### Growth

-   Designed to remain stable for **10--20 years**

#### Zero SA2s

Nil population areas such as: - Ports, airports - Water bodies -
Industrial zones - National parks

## Statistical Area Level 3 (SA3)

SA3s are built from SA2s and represent regional-level geographies.

-   **Total regions:** 359

### Purpose

-   Regional analysis framework for ABS data\
-   Aggregates to SA4

### Design Criteria

#### Population

-   Typically **30,000--130,000**

#### Functional Areas

-   Represent regional cities or clusters of suburbs

#### Zero SA3s

Nil population areas such as: - Large national parks
